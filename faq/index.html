FAQ

Printable art FAQ

What is a printable?

A printable is a digital file of the artwork. You buy it once, download it, and print it yourself. No physical item is shipped: no print, no frame.

Where do I buy?

All purchases happen on our Etsy shop. Each product page on this site has a Buy on Etsy button that opens the exact listing. Prices are shown on Etsy.

What will I receive?

After payment, Etsy gives you a download with access to the high-resolution JPG files, all at 300 DPI. Most designs include several ratio folders (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 11x14 and ISO A sizes), so you can match common frame sizes. The exact list is in each product description.

How do I print?

  • Choose the file whose ratio matches your frame.
  • Print at home, at a local print shop, or through an online printing service.
  • Upload the original full-resolution file and choose fit, not fill, so nothing is cropped.
  • Matte or heavyweight art paper works well for painterly designs.

Will the colours look exactly like the mockups?

Colours can vary between screens, printers, inks and papers. Mockups show display ideas; frames and furniture are not included.

Can I use the art commercially?

No. The files are for personal use only. Please see our terms.

I need help with my order

Orders, downloads and refunds are handled through Etsy. Message us on Etsy from your purchase page, or write to us via the contact page.
