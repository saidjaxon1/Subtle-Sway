Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

This policy explains what happens to your data when you visit subtlesway.com ("the site"), run by Subtle Sway ("we", "us").

The short version

  • The site does not use cookies.
  • The site does not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • The site has no accounts, forms or checkout, so we do not collect personal information through it.
  • All purchases happen on Etsy, under Etsy's own privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts and media

Fonts, images and videos are served from this site itself. We do not load fonts or scripts from third-party services.

Etsy

When you click Buy on Etsy or any Etsy link, you leave this site. Your purchase, payment, download and any messages are handled by Etsy and governed by the Etsy Privacy Policy. As the seller, we receive the order details Etsy shares with sellers and use them only to fulfil and support your order.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK or EU GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Email us at saidjaxonshodmonov@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
